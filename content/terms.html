A demo with generated data

Every meter, reading and consumer in this app is generated by the app itself. No real customer data is used. Place names are real localities, used only to make the demo realistic; the networks, figures and officer roles are fictional, and any resemblance to real consumers or staff is coincidental.

Not a finding of theft

The tool flags suspected consumption drops and recommends homes for inspection. It never determines that theft happened; only an inspection by an authorised field officer can do that.
